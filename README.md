# Adventure Works – Sales & Customer Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Adventure Works business dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, product movement, and profitability.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights using data analytics and business intelligence tools. The analysis focuses on identifying valuable customer segments, evaluating product performance, understanding sales contributions, and supporting data-driven business decisions.

The project uses **SQL, Microsoft Excel, Tableau, and Microsoft Power BI** at different stages of data exploration, preparation, analysis, and visualization.

---

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. **Customer Segmentation:** Identify valuable customer segments based on income range, profession, geographical region, and gender.

2. **Customer Contribution Analysis:** Evaluate the contribution of different customer segments to overall sales and identify potential opportunities for market expansion.

3. **Product Performance Analysis:** Classify products into fast-moving and slow-moving products based on their sales performance.

4. **Category & Subcategory Analysis:** Measure the contribution of product categories and subcategories to total sales and profit.

5. **Sales & Profitability Analysis:** Understand sales trends, profitability, and product performance to support business decisions.

6. **Business Recommendations:** Translate analytical findings into actionable recommendations for customer targeting, product management, and market expansion.

## Key Business Analysis

### 1. Customer Segmentation Analysis

Analyzed customer groups based on:

- Income range
- Profession
- Geographical region
- Gender

Evaluated the sales contribution of each customer segment to understand purchasing patterns and identify high-value customer groups.

**Business Objective:** Help the business identify valuable customer segments and potential markets for expansion.

### 2. Product Performance Analysis

Classified products into:

- Fast-moving products
- Slow-moving products

Evaluated product sales performance to understand demand and identify products with different movement patterns.

**Business Objective:** Support inventory planning, product management, and sales strategy.

### 3. Category & Subcategory Analysis

Analyzed the contribution of product categories and subcategories to:

- Total sales
- Total profit

Compared product group performance to identify major contributors to business revenue and profitability.

**Business Objective:** Understand which product categories and subcategories contribute most significantly to business performance.

# Project-DA_P1414-Adventure-works
An end-to-end analysis of the AdventureWorks sales data using SQL, Excel, Tableau and Power BI. The goal is to show which customer segments drive sales, to guide market expansion, and which products and categories drive sales and profit.
Business Objectives
Customer segmentation: Identify top customer segments by income range, profession, region and gender, and measure each segment's contribution to sales.
Product performance: Classify products as fast-moving or slow-moving, and measure category and sub-category contribution to sales and profit.
